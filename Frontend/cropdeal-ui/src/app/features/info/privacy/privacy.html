Privacy

Privacy information.

This page explains the types of information used by the CropDeal demonstration application.

Account information

CropDeal may process information entered when creating and using an account, such as your name, username, email address, phone number and account role.

Marketplace information

Farmers may provide shop and product information. Buyers may provide order and delivery information required for marketplace functionality.

Payments

Payment processing is integrated through Razorpay. CropDeal uses payment transaction information required to associate successful payments with orders.

Demonstration project

CropDeal is currently presented as a portfolio and demonstration application and should not be treated as a production commercial marketplace.
