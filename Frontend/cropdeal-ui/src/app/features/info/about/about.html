About CropDeal

Making agricultural trade simpler.

CropDeal is a digital agricultural marketplace designed to connect farmers directly with buyers.

What is CropDeal?

CropDeal allows farmers to create their own shops, list agricultural products and manage their inventory while buyers can discover produce, place orders and complete payments through a simple online marketplace.

For farmers

Farmers can manage shops, publish crop listings, track available quantities and view confirmed customer sales from their dashboard.

For buyers

Buyers can browse marketplace listings, view product information, add products to their cart, manage delivery addresses and complete checkout securely.

Built as a full-stack platform

CropDeal demonstrates a microservice-based marketplace using Angular and Spring Boot, including service discovery, API routing, inventory management, ordering and online payment integration.
